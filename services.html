Services

Civil engineering work for contractors, consultants and developers — in Pokhara and remotely.

What I do

If you need a number you can bid on, or drawings you can build from, this is what I take on.

How I work

The same four rules apply to every job, small or large.

  • I quote a delivery day and hold to it. If something changes, you hear it from me before the deadline, not after.
  • I ask for 50% upfront on anything over NPR 15,000 or USD 100. Files are released once payment clears, or through the platform's escrow.
  • I work in DWG and PDF, and in editable Excel for takeoffs and BOQs — never a locked image of a spreadsheet.
  • If a job is outside my scope, I tell you rather than take it and fail. That has cost me work and it is still the rule.

Why me

Two things most people quoting the same job cannot say.

I have stood on the site. My takeoffs account for what actually gets wasted and what actually gets missed — not only what the drawing says. Two years supervising ten-storey buildings teaches you where estimates go wrong.

I write code. When you send me a repetitive estimating job, I can build you a tool instead of doing it by hand twice. That is the difference between hiring a drafter and hiring someone who leaves you with something reusable.
